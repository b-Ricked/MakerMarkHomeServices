1-Year Limited Labor Guarantee

Maker Mark Home Services stands firmly behind the quality of our labor. We provide a One (1) Year Limited Labor Warranty from the date of project completion. If a structural adjustment or hardware mount fails strictly due to installation error, we will return to repair or re-secure the installation at zero additional cost to you.

What Is Excluded From Coverage?

This warranty covers our manual labor quality exclusively. It does not cover:

  • Manufacturer manufacturing defects inherent to user-supplied hardware or smart electronics.
  • Failures caused by structural shifting of the home, settling walls, plumbing leaks, or severe weather conditions.
  • Normal wear and tear or misuse by the customer after installation.

How to Report a Warranty Claim

If you suspect a workmanship defect within one year of completion, please contact us via phone or email with photos describing the issue. We will evaluate the claim and respond within 2-3 business days with our assessment and remedy timeline.
